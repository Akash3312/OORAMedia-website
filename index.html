How Oora Media Works

One network. Three groups that benefit.

Venues provide the screens, advertisers provide the messages, and Oora Media brings them together.

Step 1

Screen Partner

A café, gym, salon or other venue provides access to a suitable digital screen they already have.

Step 2

Oora Media

We build the network of screens and connect them with local advertising opportunities — handling the coordination in between.

Step 3

Advertiser

Local businesses promote themselves to relevant audiences in the places those people already spend time.

Step 4

Revenue Opportunity

Where advertising revenue is generated, it can be shared according to the agreed partnership arrangement.

Common Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Do I need to buy a new screen?

In most cases, no. Oora Media is built around screens venues already have. We'll talk through your setup to see whether it's suitable.

Will ads replace my own content?

No. Your own content stays on screen. Advertising uses an agreed share of screen time that we discuss with you up front.

How much could a Screen Partner earn?

It depends on your location, audience, and advertiser demand. Any figures we discuss are estimates only — actual advertising income may vary and is not guaranteed.

Can advertisers book and pay online?

Not yet. Right now advertisers submit an enquiry and we help coordinate the campaign directly. Online booking is something we plan to explore as the network grows.

About Oora Media

Building a Smarter Local Advertising Network.

Oora Media connects advertisers with real customers through digital screens in the places they already spend their time, and engage every day. We help bring your message directly into the real-world spaces your customers already visit.

Our Approach

Starting locally, in Adelaide.

We're beginning right here in Adelaide, working with a small number of founding Screen Partners and advertisers to test the model properly before we expand.

That means listening closely to venue owners and local businesses, getting the details right, and growing at a pace that keeps the network useful for everyone involved.

What We Stand For

Three simple principles.

Local first

Local venues, local advertisers, local audiences. We start in our own backyard and build from there.

Fair to both sides

Clear, honest arrangements that work for Screen Partners and advertisers alike — no overpromising.

Built to grow carefully

We test, learn and improve with founding partners before expanding the network further.
